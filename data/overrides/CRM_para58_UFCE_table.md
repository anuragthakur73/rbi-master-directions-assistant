Table 1: Incremental Provisioning and Capital Requirements for UFCE, by ratio of Potential Loss to EBID:
- Potential Loss / EBID up to 15 per cent: incremental provisioning 0; incremental capital 0.
- Potential Loss / EBID more than 15 per cent and up to 30 per cent: incremental provisioning 20 bps; incremental capital 0.
- Potential Loss / EBID more than 30 per cent and up to 50 per cent: incremental provisioning 40 bps; incremental capital 0.
- Potential Loss / EBID more than 50 per cent and up to 75 per cent: incremental provisioning 60 bps; incremental capital 0.
- Potential Loss / EBID more than 75 per cent: incremental provisioning 80 bps; incremental capital requirement of a 25 percentage point increase in the risk weight.
