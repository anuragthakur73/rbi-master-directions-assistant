Table of Loan to Value (LTV) ratios and Risk Weights for housing finance (applies to loans not covered by the proviso below, i.e. loans sanctioned after June 06, 2017):

(a) Individual Housing Loans
- Loan up to ₹30 lakh, LTV ratio up to 80%: risk weight 35%.
- Loan up to ₹30 lakh, LTV ratio above 80% and up to 90%: risk weight 50%.
- Loan above ₹30 lakh and up to ₹75 lakh, LTV ratio up to 80%: risk weight 35%.
- Loan above ₹75 lakh, LTV ratio up to 75%: risk weight 50%.

(b) CRE-RH (Commercial Real Estate – Residential Housing, defined at paragraph 131): LTV ratio not applicable; risk weight 75%.

Provided that the LTV ratios and Risk Weights for claims secured by residential property for loans sanctioned till June 06, 2017 shall be as set out below (older table, for loans sanctioned up to June 06, 2017 only):

(a) Individual Housing Loans
- (i) Loan up to ₹30 lakh, LTV ratio up to 80%: risk weight 35%.
- (i) Loan up to ₹30 lakh, LTV ratio above 80% and up to 90%: risk weight 50%.
- (ii) Loan above ₹30 lakh and up to ₹75 lakh, LTV ratio up to 75%: risk weight 35%.
- (ii) Loan above ₹30 lakh and up to ₹75 lakh, LTV ratio above 75% and up to 80%: risk weight 50%.
- (iii) Loan above ₹75 lakh, LTV ratio up to 75%: risk weight 75%.

(b) Commercial real estate – residential housing (CRE-RH): LTV ratio not applicable; risk weight 75%.

(c) Commercial Real Estate (CRE): LTV ratio not applicable; risk weight 100%.
